# Empo name and logo

The name "Empo", the Empo logo, and the Empo icon identify the app that Mateo M. publishes. They are not under the GPL. Mateo M. keeps all rights to them.

These files contain the Empo logo and the Empo icon:

- `ios/Empo/Assets.xcassets/AppIcon.appiconset/`
- `ios/Empo/Assets.xcassets/EmpoMark.imageset/`
- `docs/media/empo-icon.png`
- `landing/media/icon.png`
- `public/icon.png`
- `public/share.png`

## Permitted uses

- Say in text that your work is "based on Empo" or "a fork of Empo".
- Use the name and the icon to write or talk about Empo, for example in a review or an article.
- Keep the files in an unchanged copy of this repository, for example a fork on GitHub.

## Uses that are not permitted

- Publish an app, a store listing, or a website with the Empo name, the Empo logo, or the Empo icon.
- Use a name, a logo, or an icon that people can confuse with Empo.
- Use the name, the logo, or the icon in a way that says or implies that Mateo M. made, approves, or supports your work.

Before you publish a modified version, replace the name, the logo, and the icon.

To ask for a different use, open an issue at https://github.com/mateo-m/empo-app/issues.
