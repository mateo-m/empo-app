Empo is a game launcher for iPhone and iPad. It runs games that were made for RPG Maker XP,
VX, and VX Ace, and for modern [mkxp-z](https://github.com/mkxp-z/mkxp-z) forks such as
Pokemon Essentials fangames. Under the launcher is
[mkxp-z-apple-mobile](https://github.com/mateo-m/mkxp-z-apple-mobile), an iOS fork of the
mkxp-z engine.

The name comes from _emporos_, ancient Greek for a traveler who rides on someone else's ship.

## What Empo does

- It plays games that target RGSS1 (XP), RGSS2 (VX), RGSS3 (VX Ace), and modern mkxp-z forks.
- It ships three Ruby interpreters (1.8, 1.9, and 3.1) in one binary. Each game runs on the
  Ruby version that it targets. See [Multi-Ruby](/multi-ruby).
- It imports games from folders or from archives (`.zip`, `.7z`, `.rar`, JoiPlay's `.jgp`, and
  self-extractable `.exe`).
- It draws a D-pad and action buttons on the screen. You can move them, and you can keep a
  different layout for each game and each orientation.
- It pauses a game, and it resumes the game later from the library.
- It sorts, searches, and lists your library in a grid or in a list.

## Status

Empo is pre-release. It is not on the App Store. The app works from end to end with RGSS1,
RGSS2, and RGSS3 games, and with modern mkxp-z forks.

Compatibility reports for single games are welcome. Open an issue on
[GitHub](https://github.com/mateo-m/empo-app/issues), or ask on
[Discord](https://discord.gg/m3YnpXMxrB).
