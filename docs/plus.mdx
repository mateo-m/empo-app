Empo Plus is a paid membership that supports the work on Empo. It also adds extra features.
I build Empo alone in my spare time, and members pay for that time.

Empo stays free to play. You do not need Empo Plus to import games, play them, or keep your saves.

## What you get

Empo Plus adds more places to back up your saves:

| Place          | Without Empo Plus | With Empo Plus |
| -------------- | ----------------- | -------------- |
| iCloud Drive   | Yes               | Yes            |
| Dropbox        | No                | Yes            |
| Google Drive   | No                | Yes            |
| S3 storage     | No                | Yes            |
| WebDAV server  | No                | Yes            |

To add a place, open **Settings → Backups** and tap **Add a location**.

Later versions of Empo will add more features to Empo Plus.

## Join

1. Join Empo on [Patreon](https://www.patreon.com/empo_app) with a paid membership.
2. In Empo, open **Settings → Empo Plus**.
3. Tap **Already a member? Sign in**, and sign in with your Patreon account.

If Empo says that your account is not a member yet, wait until Patreon shows your membership.
Then tap **Check again**.

## Devices

Empo Plus works on up to 3 devices at a time for each Patreon account. If you sign in on a fourth
device, Empo shows **Sign out other devices**. Tap it to sign out the other devices and use
Empo Plus on this one.

## When your membership ends

Backups to iCloud Drive continue. Backups to Dropbox, Google Drive, S3 storage, and WebDAV
servers stop. Your backups stay where they are, and you can still restore your saves from them.

## Sign out

Open **Settings → Empo Plus** and tap **Sign out of Patreon**. Empo deletes your sign-in from the
device and from its server.

## Privacy

Empo's server reads only your Patreon user ID and whether you have a paid membership. It does not
read your name, your email address, or your payment details. The
[privacy policy](/privacy) gives the full details.

## App Store builds

A build of Empo from the App Store can also sell Empo Plus as a monthly subscription. Empo is not
on the App Store yet, so today Patreon is the only way to join.
