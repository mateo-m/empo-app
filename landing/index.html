It looks like the game. Because it is the game.

Three taps from a zip file to a title screen.

Empo ships no games. You bring the ones you already have.

  1. Import

    Share a folder, a zip, a 7z, a rar, or a JoiPlay package to Empo.

  2. Play

    Empo picks the Ruby the game needs. Move the D-pad and the buttons where you want them.

  3. Pause

    Freeze the game, go back to the library, and tap the card to return where you left off.

Here be dragons, or bugs!

Empo is pre-release and not on the App Store. I am a lone dev who builds this in my spare time. Save often.

  • You sideload it. AltStore, SideStore, or Sideloadly. iOS 26 or newer.
  • One game per session. Close Empo from the app switcher before you start another one.
  • Ogg and Theora movies only. An MP4 opening stays blank. The game still runs.
